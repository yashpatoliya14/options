# Strategy & Engine Details

> [!NOTE]
> This document explains the trading logic, signal generation, option selection, and risk management happening inside the `strategy/` directory and `engine.py`.

## The Supertrend Indicator

The core directional bias is generated by the **Supertrend** indicator, located in `strategy/supertrend.py`.
- **Timeframe:** Evaluated on 3-Hour (3H) candles.
- **Parameters:** ATR Period = 16, Multiplier = 1.5.
- **No Look-ahead Bias:** A signal is only evaluated on **closed** candles. The engine never reacts to a forming candle, which prevents false signals and look-ahead bias in backtests.

## Signal Processing & Trade Execution

`engine.py` processes candles sequentially. When a closed candle is received:
1. **Indicator Update:** The Supertrend state is updated.
2. **Signal Change Detection:** 
   - If the trend flips from `BEARISH` to `BULLISH` (a `BUY` signal): The engine attempts to close any open CALLs and sell a PUT.
   - If the trend flips from `BULLISH` to `BEARISH` (a `SELL` signal): The engine attempts to close any open PUTs and sell a CALL.
   - If the trend remains the same: The position is held. Premium fluctuations do NOT cause an exit unless a stop-loss is triggered.
3. **Higher Timeframe (HTF) Filter:** The engine tracks a larger timeframe (e.g., 1-Day) Supertrend. Trades can be skipped if the 3H signal contradicts the 1D trend (configurable).

## Option Selection

When a trade is triggered, `strategy/option_selector.py` and `strategy/expiry_selector.py` determine the exact contract:
- **Direction:** `BUY` signal = Sell Put. `SELL` signal = Sell Call.
- **Strike Price:** The system selects the strike price closest to the **Supertrend reference price** (the underlying close price at the time of the signal), aiming for At-The-Money (ATM) or slightly Out-Of-The-Money (OTM) options.
- **Expiry:** The system targets Zero-DT (same day) expirations. If the premium for today's expiry is too low, it rolls to tomorrow's expiry.
- **Minimum Premium Filter:** If the selected option does not offer a minimum required premium (e.g., $250-$300 configurable in `config.py`), the trade is **skipped**.

## Risk Management & Position Sizing

Position sizing is handled dynamically by `strategy/risk_manager.py`:
- **Margin Budgeting:** Instead of a fixed lot size, you define a `MARGIN_BUDGET_USD` (e.g., $50).
- The system asks the broker for the estimated margin requirement per lot for the selected option.
- It then calculates how many lots it can sell without exceeding the margin budget.

> [!WARNING]
> Margin calculations on Delta Exchange can be complex. The current `estimate_margin_per_lot` is an approximation (Premium + 15-20% of notional). Ensure you have adequate buffer in your account.

## Stop Loss & Trade Exits

`engine.py` manages risk on open positions via `evaluate_stop_loss()`:
- **Stop Loss:** A percentage-based stop loss on the premium. Since we are selling options, if the premium *increases* by X% (e.g., 20%), the position is closed to prevent unbounded losses.
- **Trailing Breakeven:** If the option loses a significant portion of its value (theta decay in our favor), the stop loss can automatically trail to the entry price (breakeven), locking in a risk-free trade.
- **Expiration Settlement:** If a trade is held until its expiration time (assumed 12:00 UTC for Delta), the engine marks it as auto-settled.
